12 · Errors and offline · Round 11 · approved (D58)
Board only. The rule: keep what the reader already has on screen, say plainly what happened in one line, and offer one way forward. Full-screen states use the website’s illustrations and voice; small failures use the existing toast with Retry.
Offline, with stories
Banner
Offline with cache. Home still shows the stories already on the phone. A dark pill above the tab bar says so, with Retry; it goes away by itself when the connection is back. Saved stories open normally.
Action failed
Toast
Toast. A save, follow or subscribe that can’t reach the server: the same toast as Undo, with Retry. Saves and follows are kept on the phone and sync later, so most never need this.
Offline, nothing yet
Full screen
Offline, empty. First launch, or a list that has never loaded, with no connection. Girl-on-planet illustration from the website. Go to Saved only shows when there are saved stories.
Couldn’t load
Full screen
Server error. Our side failed (Sanity or the app API). Says so without codes. Try again retries; after three tries it adds a link to the status page if Be Giant has one.
Story not found
From a link
Not found. A shared link or a notification for a story that was moved or unpublished. Mirrors the website’s 404 page: illustration, “doesn’t exist yet”, then the latest stories.
Story not downloaded
Article
Article offline. Opened offline and never downloaded: the card content shows, and the body is replaced by one message. Save it for later saves the story so it downloads when the phone is back online.
Video can’t play
Video
Video. The film fails to load (offline, or YouTube or Mux is down). The poster stays dimmed behind the message; Read the story opens the article.
