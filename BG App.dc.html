00 · Home hero · pick one
Four ways to show more of the photo. The live app uses 6d.
6a
Photo, then headline
The photo runs full width with only a light scrim behind the status bar. The headline, dek and byline sit below it on white. Nothing covers the image.
6b
Short scrim
Full-height photo. The scrim covers only the bottom third, with the tag, a smaller headline and the byline. The dek moves into the article.
6c
Cream caption card
Full-height photo with no bottom scrim. The headline sits in a cream floating card, like the featured story on begiant.ca.
6d
Highlight boxes
From the website's new full-bleed hero (HeroFullBleed.tsx). The photo pins in place while the headline, dek and byline scroll up over it in white boxes, one per line. At rest only the headline overlaps the photo, and the copy stops 48px above the photo's bottom edge. Scroll to see it.
Trial · Card home · board only
Card home
Tap a story
Stack. Cards stick under the status bar and are pushed back (scale 94%, dimmed) as the next card slides over. Tall cards scroll through first, then stick. Tap any story card to open the article.
Card article
Scroll to read
Article. Same card language as Home: photo first as the hero, then the headline card, body in one white card with pull quotes, topics to follow, then quick reads and the next story. The red pie at the top fills as you read.
01 · Onboarding · topics only
Welcome
First launch
Globe (1b). 32 story covers on a slowly turning sphere that assembles from its centre; back covers small and faint, front ones solid. Drag to spin. Logo and copy centred on white; headline in two lines that rise in 120ms apart, dek lines from 1.4s, buttons last at 2.1s. Get started throws the sphere outward, then Topics.
Topics
Step 1 of 3
Motion. A chip presses to 0.95, fills ink in 150ms and a check grows in from zero width (250ms), so the label slides right. The button counts down to three, then reads Continue with the total.
Notifications
Step 2 of 3
Motion. Light, Balanced and Everything set the switches below; the ink block slides 320ms. Turning a type on drops a matching sample notification in from the top. Topics expands into one switch per topic. The weekly estimate updates live. Turn on shows the iOS permission alert.
Create account
Step 3 of 3 · main action
One tap first. Apple (black, per Apple) and Google lead; email sits third as a quiet outline. The setup summary above the buttons shows what the account keeps, so signing up reads as saving work, not starting a form. No fields on this screen.
About you
After Apple or Google
Account already made. Name and email come from Apple or Google and are shown, not asked. Three optional answers: city (one tap with Use location), age range and gender as chips, so nothing needs typing. Finish or Skip both land on Home; anything left blank becomes the unfinished-profile strip (D25).
Email sign-up
After Continue with email
Full form, only for those who pick it. Name, age, city, email and password with the storage disclaimer, unchanged from before. Back returns to the one-tap options.
01b · Skipped onboarding · guest
Soft gate: every action finishes first, then a half sheet asks. One ask per session. Not now snoozes asks for 7 days; after three dismissals only the avatar asks. Account asks: first share, second save, third follow, first milestone.
Guest Home
Try it · live
Skip anywhere in onboarding and you land here. Reading, saving and following all work without an account. The avatar turns into a guest icon and offers a free account. Open and close the lead story to see the topic nudge.
For You, no topics
Empty state
If the reader skipped topics, For You becomes the topic picker: pick 3 to build it. No account needed. Building it counts as the third follow, so the account ask can follow.
First follow
Notification ask
If they skipped notifications, the first follow asks for them once, in context. Turn on brings up the iOS alert. It never shows at the same time as an account ask.
Topic nudge
After 3 reads
After three stories in the same unfollowed topic, a cream card offers to follow it. Shown once per topic, gone after 9 seconds.
First share
Account ask
The ask waits until the share sheet has closed, so it never gets in the way of sharing.
Second save
Account ask
The save is done first, then the sheet explains what an account adds: saves on every device.
Third follow
Account ask
Fires when topics plus writers followed reaches three, from any follow button or the For You picker.
First milestone
Account ask
Fires 1.8s after the confetti on the 10-story card: keep your reading record.
Create account
From any ask
The onboarding form, opened on its own with a close button. Email completes the profile. Apple or Google skip age and city, which leaves the profile unfinished.
Unfinished profile
Banner
A cream strip under the header: what is missing and how far along. Tap opens Account details. Dismissing it hides it for good.
After dismissing
Avatar dot
A red dot stays on the avatar, and Finish your profile tops the account sheet until the profile is complete.
02 · Home and For You
Home · the big story
Tab · Home
Motion. The photo drifts at 0.4× scroll speed and the cover copy fades by 300px. Tapping the cover grows the photo into the article hero (440ms).
Jump back in
New
Behaviour. Appears after the lead story when a reader left a story between 10% and 90% read in the last 7 days. Shows minutes left. Tap reopens the story where they stopped; X dismisses it. Finishing the story clears it.
Header turns solid
Scrolled
Motion. Over the last 50px of the cover, the header blends from clear to 86% white with a 20px blur, its type from white to ink, and the website's 1px header shadow fades in. The status bar flips dark at the halfway point.
For You
Swipe left from Home
Motion. The pager follows your finger 1:1 and settles in 320ms; the underline crossfades between labels. Edit topics reopens the topic picker.
End of For You
Scroll-triggered
Motion. Within 240px of the end, five squares fill one by one (90ms stagger), then the message rises 10px and fades in. Scrolling back up resets it so it replays. The button opens a random story.
Pull to refresh
Looping · drag to try
Motion. The page moves at half your finger's distance and a 2px line fills to the 60px threshold, where the label pulses once and reads Release. Loading holds at 64px with a looping sweep. New stories fade into the list with a red New label. Touch the phone to stop the loop and drag the cover down.
Swipe to save
Gesture
Motion. An ink panel shows through as the row moves. Past 96px the icon scales to 1.15. Release slides the row home (250ms) and shows a Saved toast with View. Swipe is the only way to save from a list; the article keeps its Save tool.
Tab bar minimises
Looping · floating only
Motion. iOS 26 behaviour. Scrolling down shrinks the glass bar to the active tab, its highlight becoming a circle that matches Search (380ms), and the audio player slides into the gap beside Search. Scrolling up or tapping the bar restores it. Switching tabs stretches the selection like liquid before it settles (460ms).
End of Home · milestone
10, 25, 50, 100
Behaviour. Two modules rotate under “You’re up to date”: this card and Send a story. At 10, 25, 50, 100 stories the card becomes an award with confetti (tap the card to replay). The white panel ranks the reader against others; the dots count stories to the next milestone (red dot is the goal). The card is the share image.
End of Home · overview
Between milestones
Behaviour. Between milestones the same card shows an overview: stories finished, minutes read, top topic and the reader’s rank in it. No confetti.
End of Home · send a story
Rotates
Behaviour. Suggests a story the reader finished. The link opens in the app, or the App Store if the friend doesn’t have it. A quiet “Rate it on the App Store” link sits under every module.
Rating prompt
iOS system
Behaviour. Apple’s own review prompt, asked after the third finished story and never more than Apple allows (3 times a year). Picking stars swaps Not Now for Cancel and Submit.
03 · Article · the tab bar becomes reading tools
Hero
Opened from Home
Motion. The hero arrives from the tapped image (shared element, 440ms) and the text fades in. In the bar, tabs drop 10px and fade while six tools fade in, in the same place.
Body and progress
Scrolled
Motion. Past the hero, a 50px frosted strip fades in behind the status bar with the 2px progress line on its edge. No title. Scrolling down hides the tools (250ms); any upward scroll brings them back.
Text and page
Aa panel
Motion. A glass panel grows from the Aa button (0.97 to 1, 320ms). It only sets text size, across five steps, and the body resizes live. Light and dark stay on the contrast button.
Night page
Contrast
Motion. Background, text and rules crossfade over 250ms; the tools turn to dark glass with them.
Listen
Mini player
Motion. The glass player rises 16px into place above the tools. Three bars pulse while audio plays. When the tools hide, the player drops to the bottom edge.
Share
Sheet
Motion. The sheet floats 8px in from the edges, as iOS 26 sheets do, and rises over 320ms on the website's mega-menu backdrop.
Follow similar topics
Looping · end of story
Motion. Replaces Filed under. Follow presses to 0.94, fills ink and grows a check (250ms); the sub-line changes to In your For You and a toast confirms. The writer can be followed from the same list.
04 · Discover · the Search tab
Discover
Tab · Search
Motion. Content rises 6px and fades in (250ms). Topics you follow carry a dot. Video tiles open the Video feed at that film.
Typing
Instant results
Motion. On focus the title collapses (250ms), Cancel slides in and the field gets an ink outline. Topics match first, then stories with the match set in bold.
Results
Submitted
Motion. Filter chips slide in under the field and scroll sideways. Results support swipe to save.
Filtered
Economy · Ideas
Motion. A chip fills ink in 150ms and the list updates immediately.
No results
Empty state
Motion. The brand illustration and message enter with fadeIn (400ms). Suggested words run a new search.
05 · Video · Feed and Grid
Feed
Tab · Video
Motion. Native scroll snap, one film per swipe. The active film drifts slowly and its progress line runs in real time. The tab bar turns to dark glass.
Tap and double tap
Paused
Motion. One tap pauses, and a glass play disc fades in. A double tap saves: a large bookmark pops in the middle (900ms) and a toast confirms. Read the story pauses the film and pushes the article.
Grid
View switch
Motion. The white pill slides between Feed and Grid (320ms) and the grid fades up. The tab bar minimises while you scroll the grid.
Feed to grid and back
Looping
Motion. A grid tile grows to full screen on black (420ms), then the feed takes over at that film. Home's Watch rail and Discover's video tiles use the same grow.
06 · Saved · offline by default
Saved
Tab · Saved
Motion. Each story shows a 2px progress line and minutes left, updated when you leave the article.
Edit and clear
New
Behaviour. Edit swaps the tab bar for an action bar. Tap rows to select, then Remove; or Clear finished, or Clear all. No confirm dialog: a 5-second Undo toast instead.
Grouped by topic
Sort
Motion. The white thumb slides between Latest, By topic and By author (320ms). The list re-enters with a 6px rise and fade (250ms).
Swipe to remove
Gesture
Motion. The same swipe in brand red. On release the row collapses in 260ms and a toast offers Undo.
Empty
First visit
Motion. A sample row swipes left and returns on a 3.4s loop to teach the gesture.
07 · Account and settings
Account sheet
From avatar
Motion. The sheet rises over 320ms. Following and Notifications open their settings screens.
Settings
Pushed
Motion. Pushes in 28px from the right as it fades (300ms). Display holds the tab bar labels switch (off by default) and the night page. The notification level reads the same names as onboarding.
Account
Settings
Behaviour. Name, age, city and email are editable. Sign-in method, password reset, sign out and delete account (Apple requires in-app deletion when sign-up exists).
Following
Settings
Behaviour. Topics use the onboarding bubbles. Writers list every byline with Follow / Following.
Notifications
Settings
Behaviour. Same level switch and toggles as onboarding, plus iOS permission status and quiet hours times.
Reading and display
Settings
Behaviour. Text size with a live sample, article page Light or Dark, video autoplay, tab bar labels, offline downloads.
Static page
Template
Template. One layout for About, Editorial standards, Privacy policy and Terms. Copy is placeholder for editorial and legal.
