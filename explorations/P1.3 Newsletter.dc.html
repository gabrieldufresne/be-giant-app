Back to board
Newsletter opt-in placements
Three places to offer the Be Giant newsletter, each with what an existing subscriber sees and what happens after someone says no. Getting an email in your inbox is a bigger commitment than visiting the website, so each option asks once, plainly, and someone who is already subscribed is never asked. Every phone below is live: tap the checkbox, Subscribe or Not now. Nothing here changes the live app. The new states only switch on when a phone is given news="a" | "b" | "c".
Recommendation
Option C, plus the Settings row for everyone
Ask after the second finished story, in a half sheet, and ask once more at most.
By that point the reader has read two full stories, so they know what they would be signing up for. That is the right moment for a bigger commitment. It keeps onboarding as short as P1.1 made it, and it follows the brief's rule that personal data is asked for gradually. Signed-in readers subscribe with one tap, because we already know their email, and we can check it against the list so existing subscribers never see the sheet. Guests can subscribe too, by typing an email, without having to create an account first. A and B both ask before the reader has read anything. B also adds the newsletter to a screen that is already all about personal details.
If Be Giant makes newsletter signups a core KPI, add A as well, but not B. A reaches the most people, because everyone who gets to the account step sees it, and C then skips anyone who ticked the box. B reaches the fewest: only readers who sign up and then stay on About you.

Shaded cells are where an option is weakest.
After someone says no
A and B: never asked again
The checkbox sits in plain view on a screen the reader has to read anyway. Leaving it unticked is a real answer, so we don't come back with a pop-up later, which would feel like a second sales pitch. From then on the newsletter is only in Settings, where the note says "We won't ask again".
C: once more at most, then never
A sheet that appears at the end of a story often gets "Not now" just because the reader wants to keep reading. So C asks one more time, at least 30 days later and after 5 more finished stories. The second ask uses different words, says it is the last one, and its button says No thanks. After that, the newsletter is only in Settings.
Rules for all three
The checkbox is never ticked for the reader: CASL needs a clear yes from them. No reader gets the newsletter ask and an account or notification ask in the same session. Turning the newsletter off in Settings counts as a no, so it is never asked again. And existing subscribers are never asked at all.
A
On the free account step
One checkbox between the intro and the sign-in buttons. It goes above the buttons, because the Apple and Google buttons go straight to the system sheet, so the box has to be seen before the reader taps one. Whatever is ticked here applies to whichever sign-in they choose.
A1
Ask
Unticked by default. The line underneath says what they get and that it is free to leave. 26px box, and the whole row is the tap target.
A2
Already subscribed
Someone who subscribed in the app already has an account, so they never see this step again. Their status lives in Settings > Account, under Newsletter, next to their email (scroll down inside the phone). The weak spot: a website subscriber setting up the app sees the box anyway, because we don't know their email until after they sign in. Ticking it again does nothing.
A3
Left unticked
Never asked again. Settings shows the newsletter turned off, with "We won't ask again" and one line on what it is. Turning it on is a single tap.
B
After the personal details
The same checkbox, placed below the fields on About you (after Apple, Google or a magic link) and on the email sign-up form. After Apple or Google we already have the email, so we can check it against the list and leave the box out for existing subscribers. The email form can't check until the reader types their address.
B1
Ask, after Apple or Google
Below city, age and gender, so it's the fourth optional thing on a screen of personal questions. Finish and Skip for now both keep whatever the box is set to.
B2
Ask, email form
Below the password, above the privacy line and Create account. If the address turns out to be on the list already, ticking the box does nothing.
B3
Already subscribed
The Apple or Google email is already on the list, so About you has no checkbox and no message about it. They can see their status in Settings > Account (as in A2).
B4
Left unticked
Same as A: never asked again, and the newsletter is only in Settings.
C
After the second finished story
Recommended
A half sheet that slides up once the reader reaches the end of their second finished story, built like the existing account and notification asks: a kicker with an icon, a serif headline, one line, a black button and Not now. It never sits on top of the story. It comes up after the story ends, and not in a session where another ask has already shown. In these phones, one story already counts as finished, so the sheet appears at the end of this one. After Not now, open Your next read and finish it to see the one re-ask.
C1
Ask, signed in
One tap: the sheet shows the address it will go to. The line says why we are asking now ("You've finished two stories"). Not now closes it and allows one more ask later. Tapping the backdrop counts as Not now.
C2
Ask, guest
Guests type an email; no account is needed. We have not checked the address yet, so we send a confirmation email and the toast says so. The account ask (P1.4) waits for another session.
C3
Already subscribed
No sheet: the story ends exactly as it does today. Their status is in Settings > Account (as in A2), and the Newsletter row in Settings says Subscribed.
C4
The one re-ask
This only comes after a Not now, at least 30 days and 5 finished stories later. It has a new headline, says it is the last ask, and the button says No thanks. After this, the reader is never asked again.
C5
Said no twice
The sheet never comes back. Settings shows the newsletter turned off, with "We won't ask again". This is the same screen as A3 and B4, so every option ends up in the same place.
Where status lives
Settings > Account, in a Newsletter section above Sign-in. It goes in Account rather than Notifications because the newsletter goes to the reader's email address, which is shown on that page, while Notifications is only about alerts on the phone. Settings already has a static Newsletter row in its Be Giant group. With news set, that row shows Subscribed or Off and opens Account. A toast confirms every change. Turning the newsletter off says plainly that the account, topics and notifications are not affected.
