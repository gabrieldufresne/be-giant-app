# P3.1 For You icon and followed-tag cue

Exploration: `explorations/P3.1 For You icon.dc.html` (serve the repo, open `/explorations/P3.1%20For%20You%20icon.dc.html`). Nothing in the live app has changed.

## Problem

D28 marks For You and followed tags with `assets/light/sparkle.svg`: a four-point star with small sparks. That's the shape Gemini and most AI assistants use to mean "AI did this", so it tells readers their feed is generated. We want it to say the opposite: picked because of what you follow.

The sparkle is used in seven kinds of place today (14 instances in `BeGiantAppPhone.dc.html`): the For You segment of the Home / For You switch, the followed-tag marker (Home lead, Home lists, Latest, article header, article "next"), the For You reason line, "Show me a random story", "For you today", the guest follow ask and Settings > Following. All of them should change together.

## Options (restrained to playful)

Each option is shown in the exploration at real size: the Home / For You switch (44px, 100px segments, 13px icon) in both states on the solid white header, over the hero photo, and on a dark surface, plus the reason line and a followed tag in a For You row, light and dark, at 12px.

| 01 | Person and page | Phosphor `book-open-user` | Your reading | Low | Muddy at 12 to 13px; better for a larger slot |
| 02 | Compass | Phosphor `compass` | Finding your way | Low | Means discovery, which is the Discover tab's job |

## Top two

1. **Heart (ships now).** The clearest personal signal, crisp at 12 and 13px in all three contexts, with no AI association. It's already in Phosphor, so it can replace the sparkle everywhere in one pass. Condition: no likes on stories, so the heart keeps meaning "what you follow".
2. **Hand-drawn star (needs Maya).** The most ownable: it brings Maya's illustration voice into the app chrome and keeps the star that readers already link to follows (D28). It beats the heart only if it's drawn well. Until then, the Phosphor five-point star is a safe stand-in. It reads as "favourites", not AI.

The person-and-page icon is a good fit for larger moments (For You empty state, the follow ask) even if it doesn't win the small slots.

## AI-symbol risk

- **High: the current sparkle.** Replace it in all seven kinds of place, including "Show me a random story" and "For you today".
- **High: any four-point star**, even alone and without sparks. Rule it out of the Maya brief.
- **Watch: hand-drawn star.** Low risk as one five-point star. It drifts toward AI if it gets extra sparks, a glow, a gradient or a twinkle animation.
- **Watch: hand and heart.** No AI risk, but it clashes in meaning with donate.
- **Low:** heart, compass, person and page, bookmark-heart.

## What needs Maya

- **Hand-drawn star (pick 2).** One five-point star in her line, slightly uneven, open centre. No companion sparks, no four-point shape, no glow. Delivered as a single-colour SVG at Phosphor light stroke weight on a 256 grid, so it can be tinted and inverted like the other icons, plus a fill version for active states. It must hold up at 12px.
- **Bookmark-heart hybrid (only if wanted).** One bookmark with a small heart cut into or sitting on it, same weight. Worth commissioning only if Save moves to a different mark. Otherwise it reads as Save.

Both are shown in the exploration as dashed "Illustration: Maya" placeholders, with a Phosphor stand-in at small sizes.
