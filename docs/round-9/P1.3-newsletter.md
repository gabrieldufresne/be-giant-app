# P1.3 Newsletter opt-in placements

Round 9, prompt P1.3. Exploration page: `explorations/P1.3 Newsletter.dc.html`, with 13 live phones. Nothing changes in the live app until an option is picked. The new states only switch on when a phone is given `news="a" | "b" | "c"`. This is PRD Q7, "Where does newsletter signup live?". The newsletter block was removed from articles in R1 (D9).

The repo doesn't give the newsletter a name, so the copy calls it "the Be Giant newsletter". Because we don't know how often it goes out, the copy doesn't promise a schedule. It says "Our reporting from across Canada, by email. Free, and you can unsubscribe anytime."

## Recommendation

**Option C, plus the Settings row for everyone.** Ask once, in a half sheet, after the reader finishes their second story. Ask once more at most.

- **The reader knows what they're getting.** By then they've read two full stories, which is the right moment to ask for a bigger commitment than a website visit.
- **Onboarding stays short.** P1.1 kept it at a welcome screen and three steps, and C adds nothing to it.
- **It follows the brief.** The shared brief says personal data is asked for gradually, never all at once.
- **It's one tap for signed-in readers.** We already know their email, so the sheet shows the address and a Subscribe button.
- **Existing subscribers never see it.** We can check a signed-in reader's email against the list.
- **Guests can subscribe too.** They type an email and don't need an account.

**If Be Giant makes newsletter signups a core KPI, add A as well, but not B.** A reaches the most people, because everyone who gets to the account step sees it. C then skips anyone who ticked the box. B reaches the fewest: only readers who sign up and then stay on About you.

## The three options

| | A. Account step | B. After personal details | C. After the second story |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | Create your free account, between the intro and the sign-in buttons | About you (after Apple, Google or a magic link), below the fields; and the email form, below the password | Half sheet at the end of the second finished story |
| Who sees it | Everyone who reaches the account step | Only readers who create an account and stay on About you | Every reader who finishes two stories, guests included |
| Has read anything yet | No | No | Yes |
| Knows if already subscribed | No (the ask comes before sign-in) | Yes after Apple, Google or a link; no on the email form | Yes when signed in; for guests, checked when they submit |
| Cost to onboarding | One more decision on the step that carries the most | A fourth optional item on a screen of personal questions | None |
| Re-ask | Never | Never | Once more, then never |

### A. Checkbox on the free account step

- The checkbox goes above the sign-in buttons. The Apple and Google buttons go straight to the system sheet, so the box has to be seen before the reader taps one. Whatever is ticked applies to whichever sign-in they choose.
- The box is never ticked for the reader (CASL). It is 26px with a 6px radius, and the whole row is the tap target. The label reads "Send me the Be Giant newsletter", with one line underneath.
- **Already subscribed.** Readers who subscribed in the app already have an account, so they never see this step again. Their status is in Settings > Account. The weak spot is a website subscriber setting up the app: they see the box anyway, because we don't know their email until after they sign in. Ticking it again does nothing.
- **Dismissed.** Leaving the box unticked counts as a no, and the reader is never asked again. Settings shows the newsletter turned off, with "We won't ask again".

### B. Checkbox after the personal data fields

- It is the same checkbox. On About you it sits below city, age and gender, and Finish and Skip for now both keep whatever it is set to. On the email form it sits below the password, above the privacy line and Create account.
- **Already subscribed.** After Apple, Google or a magic link we already have the email. If it is on the list, About you has no checkbox and no message about it. The email form can't check until the reader types their address, so the box stays, and ticking it does nothing.
- **Dismissed.** As in A: never asked again, and the newsletter is only in Settings.

### C. One-time prompt after the second finished story

- The sheet follows the existing account and notification asks:
  - an envelope icon and a NEWSLETTER kicker
  - a serif headline: "Get our reporting by email"
  - one line that says why we're asking now: "You've finished two stories…"
  - a black Subscribe button and Not now
  - a footer line: "Free. Unsubscribe anytime, from any email or in Settings."
- **Signed in:** the sheet shows "Sent to" with the reader's address, so subscribing is one tap. The toast says "You're subscribed to the newsletter".
- **Guest:** an email field replaces the address. Because the address hasn't been checked, we send a confirmation email, and the toast says "Check your email to confirm".
- **When it appears:** after the end of the second finished story, never on top of a story. It never appears in a session where an account or notification ask has already shown (we propose this rule to P1.4). Tapping the backdrop counts as Not now.
- **Already subscribed:** no sheet. The story ends exactly as it does today.
- **Dismissed once:** asked once more, at least 30 days later and after 5 more finished stories. The second ask has a different headline ("Still want the newsletter?"), says it is the last one ("We won't ask again. It's always in Settings."), and its button says No thanks.
- **Dismissed twice:** never asked again, and the newsletter is only in Settings.

## Re-ask rule, and why

- **A and B: never ask again.** The checkbox sits in plain view on a screen the reader has to read anyway. Leaving it unticked is a real answer. Coming back with a pop-up later would feel like a second sales pitch to an audience that skews 55+.
- **C: ask once more at most, then never.** A sheet that appears at the end of a story often gets "Not now" just because the reader wants to keep reading. One more ask, spaced well apart and clearly marked as the last, respects that without nagging.
- **Rules for all three:**
  - Turning the newsletter off in Settings counts as a no, so it is never asked again.
  - Existing subscribers are never asked.
  - The newsletter ask never shows in the same session as an account or notification ask.

## Settings (all options)

**Settings > Account gets a Newsletter section, above Sign-in.**

- The row reads "The Be Giant newsletter", with a switch.
- When subscribed, the sub-line says "Sent to maya.okafor@gmail.com". The note says that turning it off stops the newsletter only, and that the account, topics and notifications stay as they are.
- When not subscribed, the sub-line says "Not subscribed". After a no, the note says "We won't ask again. Turn it on here anytime" and gives the one-line description.
- A toast confirms every change.

It goes in Account rather than Notifications because the newsletter goes to the email address shown on that page, while Notifications is only about alerts on the phone. Settings already has a static "Newsletter · Subscribed" row in its Be Giant group. With `news` set, that row shows Subscribed or Off and opens Account. In the live app the row is still static. **When an option ships, that row should become live as well.**

## Open questions

- **Is newsletter signup a core KPI?** The answer decides between C alone and A plus C. We recommend against B either way.
- **Which list, and how often?** Is there one newsletter or several (a daily brief, a weekly, regional or topic lists)? If there are several, the Settings row becomes a short list of switches, and C offers the list closest to the story the reader just finished. The copy will name the schedule once we know it.
- **Does the app need double opt-in for CASL?**
  - **What CASL needs:** express consent, which means a box the reader ticks themselves (never ticked for them) or a clear button. Every email must also give the sender's name and contact details and a way to unsubscribe, and unsubscribes must be processed within 10 business days.
  - **Double opt-in:** CASL doesn't strictly require it. We suggest it for guest emails in C, because those addresses haven't been checked. Apple, Google and magic-link addresses are already confirmed.
  - **For counsel and Field Office:** Be Giant's counsel should confirm all of this. The app also needs to record when and where each consent was given (time, screen, and the exact wording shown).
- **Apple's Hide My Email.** Sign in with Apple can share a relay address that won't match the website list, so a website subscriber may still see A, B or C once. Should we also try to match their typed email, or accept the occasional repeat?
- **Which platform runs the list?** Field Office needs the email service provider and an API that answers "is this address subscribed?". Every "already subscribed" state depends on it.
- **How often asks can appear, alongside P1.4.** We propose a shared rule: at most one ask (account, notifications or newsletter) per session, with the newsletter last in line.

## What changed in code

- **`BeGiantAppPhone.dc.html`.** Everything new is in clearly marked `P1.3` blocks and is off unless `news` is set:
  - A checkbox block on the account step (A).
  - Checkbox blocks on About you and on the email form (B).
  - A Newsletter section in Settings > Account.
  - A new half sheet after the notification ask (C).
  - One line in `onArtScroll` that only runs when `news="c"`.
  - New methods `nlSt`, `nlEnd`, `nlClose` and `newsVals`.
- **The `renderVals` wrapper.** `renderVals()` is now a one-line wrapper. It returns `baseVals()` unchanged unless `news` is set, in which case it merges `newsVals()` on top. The old method body was renamed `baseVals()` and is otherwise untouched. The wrapper is needed so the existing Settings Newsletter row can be overridden.
- **New props:**
  - `news="a" | "b" | "c"` picks the option.
  - `news-state="subscribed" | "declined" | "later"` sets the reader's status; `later` means C was dismissed once.
  - `news-read="1"` counts one story as already finished.
  - `news-open="1"` opens the C sheet.
- **`bg-app-data.js`:** one new preset, `news-guest-end` (a guest at the end of an article).
- **Exploration page.** It sets `<base href="../">`, so it loads `support.js` and the rest from the site root instead of using `../` paths. `dc-import` and the phone's own assets resolve against the page URL, so the phone only mounts this way. This works locally and on GitHub Pages.
- **Icons:** Phosphor `envelope-simple-light` and `check-light`, both already in `assets/light/`.
- **Checked:**
  - `/app.html` at 390×844 and the board look and behave as before: no checkbox, no Settings switch and no sheet render without `news`.
  - The exploration, the board and the app show no console errors.
