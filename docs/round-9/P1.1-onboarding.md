# P1.1 Onboarding and account creation

Round 9, prompt P1.1. Board row: **09b · Onboarding v2 · Round 9** (last row on the board). Every phone there is live and can be clicked through.

## The flow

The flow is a welcome screen followed by three steps. It has not grown. Every step has Skip in the top bar, and the account step also has "Not now, start reading".

| Step | Screen | Rationale (one line) |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Globe (D32), unchanged | Shows the reporting before asking for anything. |
| 1 | What would you like to follow? Three topics are pre-checked | Pre-checking meets the 3-topic target (D24) on the first tap, so following becomes a matter of editing the defaults rather than choosing from scratch. |
| 2 | Notifications (owned by P1.2) | No change from P1.1. |
| 3 | Create your free account | "Free" is in the headline and the line under it says "No payment, ever", so nobody expects a charge. The summary of choices above the buttons can still be edited. |
| 3b | About you (after Apple, Google, Microsoft or a magic link) | The account already exists at this point, so every field here is optional and Skip for now is one tap. |

### Resuming later

- Topics skipped: For You becomes the topic picker (01b row), and the third follow brings up the account ask.
- Account skipped: you become a guest, and the guest asks come up at the first share, second save, third follow and first milestone (D24).
- About you left blank: the unfinished-profile strip and the avatar dot appear (D25), and Account and settings shows "x of 4 done".

## What changed

1. **Topics.** The headline is now "What would you like to follow?" Economy, Housing and Climate start checked, and the copy says so ("We started you with three"). A rule above the chips reads **Placeholder topics · final list from Be Giant**. The 3-topic minimum stays because of D24.
2. **Account: four options.** Continue with Apple and Continue with Google are unchanged and each keeps its own brand guidelines (Apple black with the SF font, Google white with a #747775 outline and Roboto). Below an "Or with email" rule, the new magic-link button **Email me a sign-in link** sits as an outline button, and **Use a password instead** links to the existing email form. The line under the headline now reads "No payment, ever…"
3. **Account: five providers** (`start="ob-account-5"`). Brand buttons stack above the rule and the email options stay below it, so a fifth provider is one more 52px row and nothing else moves. Microsoft is drawn to its own guideline: white, #8C8C8C outline, the four-square logo, Segoe UI semibold in #5E5E5E. At 390×844 the privacy line and Not now move below the fold, so they take a short scroll.
4. **Magic link** (new). There are two states:
   - Ask: an email field, an "Email me a link" button and "Use a password instead". New and returning readers see the same screen.
   - Sent: "Check your email", the address with Change, Open Mail, and Resend link.
   In the prototype, Open Mail stands in for tapping the link and goes to About you.
5. **About you.**
   - After Apple, Google or Microsoft, name and email are filled in and marked "From Apple", "From Google" or "From Microsoft".
   - City (with Use location) and age are optional, and Skip for now stays.
   - There are two versions:
     - **A, with gender** (3 fields): `ob-details`. This is the default in the app flow.
     - **B, without gender** (2 fields): `ob-details-nogender`. **B has fewer fields.**
   - After a magic link, only the email is known, so an optional Name field is added (`ob-details-linked`).
6. New `start=` presets in `bg-app-data.js`: `ob-account-5`, `ob-link`, `ob-link-sent`, `ob-details-linked`, `ob-details-nogender`.
7. New icon: `assets/light/envelope-simple-light.svg`. This is the official Phosphor light file from `@phosphor-icons/core@2.1.1`, not a drawn icon.

## Tap counts

Each Allow, Continue or chip press counts as one tap. Typing is not counted.

| Path | Prototype taps | On a real iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| **Skip everything** (Get started, then Skip) | **2** | 2 |
| Skip each step one by one (Get started, Skip for now, Not now, Not now, start reading) | 4 | 4 |
| **Complete, minimum** (Get started, Continue, Turn on notifications, Allow, Continue with Apple, Skip for now) | **6** | 7, because the Apple sheet needs one confirm |
| Complete with every answer, version A (the above plus Use location, an age chip, a gender chip, and Finish in place of Skip) | 9 | 11, because the Apple sheet and the location permission each add one |
| Complete with every answer, version B (no gender) | 8 | 10 |
| Complete by magic link, minimum (Get started, Continue, Turn on, Allow, Email me a sign-in link, Email me a link, Open Mail, Skip for now) | 8 | 9, because tapping the link in Mail adds one, plus typing the email |

Completing takes 3 times as many taps as skipping at the minimum (6 against 2) and 4.5 times as many with every answer (9 against 2). Version B saves one tap and one decision.

## Open questions

- **Topic list.** The 16 chips are placeholders until Be Giant sends the final list. We also need to know which three should be pre-checked: the most-read topics, editorial picks, or picks by region?
- **Gender.** Do we collect it or drop it? We recommend dropping it (version B) unless Be Giant needs it for funder or reach reporting. It is the only field with no use in the product.
- **Magic link.** We need a link lifetime (the copy says 15 minutes) and to know whether the link should also open on another device or only on this phone. Field Office should also confirm the email provider and the deep-link handling.
- **Fifth provider.** Is Microsoft (or any other provider) actually wanted? The layout works either way.
- **Name after a magic link.** If the reader leaves Name blank, the account shows only an email. Should it be required, or should the reader be asked again later?
