# P3.2 Personalized greeting

Round 9 exploration. Board row **00b · Greeting · pick one** in `BG App.dc.html` (anchors `#g1` to `#g3g`). Nothing changes in the live app: the phone component only shows a greeting when it is given `greet="slide" | "line" | "bold"`, and the app never passes it.

## How it is built

- `BeGiantAppPhone.dc.html`, Home section: three blocks tagged `data-p32="greet-slide" | "greet-line" | "greet-bold"`. The slide block sits inside the 6d photo; the other two sit at the top of the white feed, above Jump back in and Latest.
- New props: `greet` (treatment), `greet-at` (`morning | afternoon | evening`, to force the time of day on the board; the app would use the device clock), `greet-run` (a counter that replays the slide animation when it changes).
- Logic lives in one helper, `greetVals()`, spread into `renderVals()` on its own line. Two keyframes, `bgGreetA` and `bgGreetB` (identical, so changing the name restarts the animation), are added at the end of the helmet styles.
- Board controls: Morning, Afternoon and Evening switch every phone in the row; Replay G1 plays the slide again.
- The hero (6d), Home header and Home / For You switch are untouched.

## Copy

| Time on device | Greeting | Bold sub-line |
|---|---|---|
| 5:00 a.m. to 11:59 a.m. | Good morning, Maya | Here’s what’s new since last night. |
| 12:00 p.m. to 4:59 p.m. | Good afternoon, Maya | Here’s what’s new since this morning. |
| 5:00 p.m. to 4:59 a.m. | Good evening, Maya | Here’s what you missed today. |

The name is the first word of the account name. Late night (midnight to 5 a.m.) stays "Good evening"; "Good night" reads as goodbye.

## The three treatments, ranked by how little they interrupt the hero

1. **G2 · Line above Latest (least interruption).** One line of Gibson, 15px medium, where the white page starts, with the date in grey on the right. It never touches the photo and never moves. The reader only meets it after the hero, so the first screen is exactly 6d. Cost: most people will barely notice it, which is also its strength.
2. **G1 · Slide over the hero.** A small white highlight box ("Good morning, Maya", serif light 22px) rises into place just above the headline, holds, then fades. It borrows the 6d box language so it reads as part of the hero, not a banner on top of it. It covers a strip of photo for about two seconds and then the hero is exactly as before. It is the only treatment that is seen on the first screen, so it is the most noticeable moment, but it is temporary.
3. **G3 · Bold welcome (most interruption, warmest).** Date eyebrow, a 36px serif greeting and a short what-is-new line at the top of the feed. The hero itself is untouched, but Latest moves down about 110px, so the first story starts lower and the page feels more like a letter than a news front. Warmest tone, highest cost to the feed.

The hero stays immersive in all three. G2 and G3 never touch it; G1 touches it briefly and leaves no trace.

## Motion: G1 slide

| Phase | Time | What happens | Easing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wait | 0 to 600ms after Home is on screen | Nothing, so the hero photo lands first | |
| In | 600 to 1,050ms (450ms) | Box rises 18px into place and fades from 0 to 100% | cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1), the app’s standard ease-out |
| Hold | 1,050 to 3,160ms (about 2.1s) | Still | |
| Out | 3,160 to 3,800ms (640ms) | Fades to 0 and drifts up 6px | cubic-bezier(.4, 0, 1, 1), ease-in |

- **Position:** left 20px, 98px above the photo’s bottom edge, which puts it about 10px above the headline’s first white box at rest. It never overlaps the headline or the Home / For You switch.
- **When it plays:** on cold launch, or when the app returns to Home after being in the background for more than 4 hours, and only when Home is the screen showing.
- **How often:** at most once per part of the day (once in the morning, once in the afternoon, once in the evening). Never on tab switches, pull to refresh or coming back from an article.
- **Interrupts:** if the reader scrolls or taps during the animation, it fades out immediately (200ms). It never blocks touches (`pointer-events: none`), so a tap on the box opens the hero story as normal.
- **Reduce Motion on:** no rise, just a 200ms fade in and out with the same hold.
- **VoiceOver:** announced once as a polite live region, then gone.
- Prototype note: the board plays it once per load and on Replay; the scroll and background rules above are for the build, not wired in the prototype.

## Signed-out (guest) readers

- All three show the time of day with no name: "Good morning". Never "Good morning, reader", "guest" or "friend"; a missing name should not feel like a missing account.
- G1 plays on the same schedule. G2 shows the greeting and date. G3 keeps the what-is-new line, which still makes sense without an account.
- No sign-up ask is attached to the greeting. The account prompts belong to the nudges (P1.4); a greeting that turns into a sales line stops feeling warm.
- Partially signed-up readers have a name from sign-in, so they get the named greeting.

## Recommendation

**G2, the line above Latest, as the default, with G1 as an optional once-a-day moment if Be Giant wants the greeting to be felt.**

- G2 keeps the 6d hero exactly as approved (D31) and still makes Home feel addressed to the reader. It costs about 40px and no motion, and it works the same for guests.
- G1 is the better "moment", and the motion is short enough that the hero stays the first thing people remember. If it ships, cap it at once per part of the day as above, so it never becomes something to wait through.
- G3 is the warmest but moves Latest down on every visit, which works against a news front for an older audience that comes to see what is new. Keep its sub-line idea ("Here’s what’s new since last night") as copy for G2 if the team wants a little more warmth.

## Open questions

- Should the greeting use the name from Apple or Google sign-in automatically, or only once the reader has confirmed it in About you?
- Is a greeting wanted on For You too, or only Home? (Assumed Home only.)
- French: "Bonjour, Maya" / "Bon après-midi" / "Bonsoir" if the app ships bilingual.

## G4 · Overlay (added after review)

Gabriel found G1 to G3 not impactful enough. G4 is the new direction: a short welcome moment over Home.

- **What it is:** a dark veil (`rgba(14,14,14,.76)` with a 10px backdrop blur) covers the whole screen, including the header and tab bar. Centred on it: the date as an eyebrow, the greeting in Serrif Condensed light at 48px, set on two lines ("Good morning," / "Maya"), and the what-is-new line under it. Guests get "Good morning" on one line.
- **Motion (3.2s):**

| Time | What happens | Easing |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 250ms | Veil fades in | linear |
| 200ms | Date rises 22px into place from a slight blur | cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1), 800ms |
| 320ms | "Good morning," rises in | same |
| 470ms | Name rises in | same |
| 760ms | What-is-new line rises in | same |
| to 2,500ms | Hold | |
| 2,500 to 3,200ms | Veil and text fade out, text drifts up 12px | cubic-bezier(.4, 0, 1, 1) |

- **Reduce Motion:** no rise or blur; the text simply appears with the veil and fades with it.
- **When it plays (for the build):** same rules as G1: cold launch or after more than 4 hours away, at most once per part of the day, never on tab switches or returning from an article. It does not block reading for long: Home is fully revealed at 3.2s. Field Office could also let a tap skip it.
- **Built as:** `greet="overlay"` on the phone component (board row 00b, G4 and G4 guest). Still opt-in; the live app does not show it yet.
