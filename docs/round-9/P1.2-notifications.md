# P1.2 Notifications and quiet hours

Round 9 · branch `claude/round-9-p1-2-notifications-f83b7c` · board row **09c · Notifications v2 · Round 9**

## Goal

Make the notification choice feel low-pressure so more people opt in. Readers skew 55+, so the step says plainly what will arrive, when it won't, and what happens if they say no.

## Rationale

- **Presets first, details second.** Most readers pick a preset and leave it. The type toggles now sit under a "Fine-tune" label below quiet hours, so the step looks like two decisions, not six.
- **Quiet hours at the top.** For this audience the fear is being woken up, not missing a story. Showing "held until morning" before the Allow tap answers that worry when it matters. It's on and pre-filled by default, and both times can be edited.
- **Say what happens next.** The line above the button tells the reader that the iPhone asks next. The iOS alert comes up once per install and looks like a system interruption, so warning them first means fewer reflex taps on Don't Allow.
- **A calm no.** The declined screen says the app works the same, keeps their choices, and shows the three-tap way back. We don't ask again on the spot.
- **One way back, in plain steps.** iOS won't show the permission alert a second time, so after a decline the only way back is iPhone Settings. The Settings screen and the declined screen use the same three steps and the same button wording.

## Build notes for Field Office

- Open the app's notification page directly with `UIApplication.openNotificationSettingsURLString` (iOS 16+). On older iOS, fall back to `openSettingsURLString`.
- Read `UNUserNotificationCenter.getNotificationSettings` on every foreground to choose the Settings state: `.notDetermined` → show the iOS alert; `.denied` → three steps + Open iPhone Settings; `.authorized` / `.provisional` → allowed row.
- Quiet hours are a server-side hold, not iOS Focus. The app sends the window and time zone with the device token, and the push service queues anything inside the window until the end time.
- Preset, per-type, per-topic and quiet-hours choices are kept when the reader declines, so turning notifications on later restores them.
