## Summary
Home is the front page: one big lead story that scrolls away (nothing pins, D11), then Jump back in (when there is one), Latest, a Watch rail, Short stories and Popular, ending on a grey surface with a caught-up message, one rotating share card and a rating link. For You sits one horizontal swipe away on the same pager: a short list where every story says why it is there (D28, D37). Guests with fewer than three topics see a topic picker instead (D26). Values below are iOS points on a 402pt-wide screen. The prototype hard-codes the lead story in the template and fills every list from `bg-app-data.js`; the build binds each list to the sources in the Data column.

## States
- **Signed in:** as above. **Guest:** same Home; saves start empty; For You is the picker until built; the nudge and account asks apply (`04-…`). **Unfinished profile:** strip, then avatar dot.
- **No Jump back in:** the row is absent and Latest moves up. **Refreshing, refreshed:** HOME-19, HOME-20.
- **Greeting:** off in the app (Q15). **Hero 6a–6c:** board reference only.
- **Loading (first launch, skeletons), error (fetch or refresh failed), offline:** Not designed. Proposal: show the last cached Home with "Updated {time}" and a quiet no-connection toast.
- **Signed-in reader with no followed topics:** Not designed (the picker shows only for guests and unfinished profiles).
- **Sections with too few stories** (fewer than three short stories, empty trending): Not designed; proposal: hide the section.

## Accessibility
- Labels in the prototype: "Account", "Following", "Dismiss"; the greeting is `aria-live="polite"`. Rows, tiles, the hero, switch segments, See all and the end buttons have no roles or labels; photos have empty alt. Build: each row is one element read as tag (and "Following"), title, author, read time; photos use Sanity `alt`.
- Swipe is the only save on lists (D13), so every row needs a VoiceOver custom action (Save, Unsave). The hint toast needs a spoken equivalent.
- Targets: switch segments 100 × 36pt inside a 44pt pill; Jump back in dismiss 36pt (fix to 44pt).
- Contrast: `#7A7A7A` meta at 12pt on white is about 4.3:1, just under AA; the 10pt red "New" is small. Dynamic Type for Home: Not specified (Aa applies to articles only).

## Data and analytics
- **Fetch plan:** one homepage query (`homepage` singleton, `heroArticle` and `shortStoriesSection` sections), one live-stories query for Latest and Short stories, one `trendingData` query for Popular, de-duplicated in the app in section order (lead, Jump back in, Latest, Short stories, Popular). Card fields as the website's `articleCard` projection. Images through the Sanity image URL builder with hotspot and crop.
- **Reader data:** followed topics and writers, saved ids, reading progress, finished count and minutes, hint and rating flags, last fetch time. Guests: device only. Signed in: Account (backend), so it follows the reader to a new phone.
- ~~"Finished"~~: resolved (D51, X-05), 90% read everywhere.
- **Missing in schema (for the change request):**
  - `shortStory.article` (SCR-03): reference to `article`; filled by editors when they add a short; without it the Watch rail tile and the Video feed's Read button (`09-video.md`) can't open the story.
  - No new field for Popular or the reader rank: Popular uses `trendingData`; the rank percentile needs an analytics service, not Sanity.
- **Proposed events:** home_view, lead_open, row_open (section, position), swipe_save and swipe_unsave (screen), swipe_hint_shown, rail_video_open, video_see_all, pull_refresh (new count), jump_back_in_open, jump_back_in_dismiss, foryou_view, foryou_open (reason type), bonus_story, feed_end_reached (home, for_you), end_card_shown (milestone, overview, send), card_share, send_story, rate_link_tap, review_prompt_shown, greeting_shown, topic_picker_built (count).
