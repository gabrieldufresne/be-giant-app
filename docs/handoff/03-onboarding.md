## Summary
A welcome followed by three steps: topics, notifications, free account (D35). It shows the reporting before asking for anything; topics are the only personalisation (D6); every step has one "Skip for now" anchored at the bottom and no Skip in the top bar. Three topics start checked so the 3-topic target is met on the first tap. The account step leads with Apple and Google, then a magic link, then a password (D33, D35). The account exists before any personal question, and About you is all optional.

## States
- **Default:** welcome, steps in order, three topics pre-checked, Balance, quiet hours on.
- **Fewer than three topics:** button disabled, "Pick N more".
- **Permission declined:** ONB-11. **Already decided (reinstall):** Unclear in prototype; native should read the current authorisation and skip the alert.
- **Signed in vs guest:** email form gives a full profile; Apple, Google or magic link stay unfinished unless age and city are given; skipping the account step makes a guest.
- **Opened on its own (from an ask):** same account screen with a close X (GATE-12).
- **Returning reader, errors, offline:** Proposed in Round 11, board section 13 (ONB-25 to ONB-32): sign in, validation, wrong password, email in use, expired link, age minimum, provider failure, no connection. Not in the app view until approved.
- **Reduce Motion:** Not designed. The prototype's board stillness (Globe stops after 2.4s) is prototype only, and the Globe ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Reduce Motion proposal: a still arrangement of covers with no spin or throw, the welcome copy shown at once, crossfades for step changes.

## Accessibility
- Targets: Back, Skip for now, I already have an account 44pt; primary and provider buttons 52pt. Below 44pt: topic and About you chips 40pt, "Use a password instead" 40pt, Change 32pt.
- Prototype labels: logo "Be Giant", Back "Back", provider buttons `role="button"` with logos hidden, About you chips `role="button"`.
- Gaps: chips need a selected trait; the progress line needs a value ("Step 2 of 3"); the Globe should be hidden from VoiceOver; "Pick N more" needs a hint. The welcome headline is `nowrap` and needs a reflow rule for Dynamic Type. Check `#7A7A7A` 11pt "Optional" tags for contrast.

## Data and analytics
- Reader data (Account (backend), or App state (device) for a guest): followed topic ids, notification preset, types, per-topic switches, quiet hours and time zone, sign-in provider, name, email (Apple relay supported), optional city, age range, gender (Q10), profile completeness.
- External: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, magic-link email and universal link, APNs, Core Location.
- **Missing in schema (for the request list):**
  1. `appSettings` singleton (new document) with `onboardingTopics` (array of references to `topic`, ordered) — filled by Be Giant editors — without it ONB-07 has no curated list or order (Q11).
  2. `appSettings.preselectedTopics` (SCR-04) (array of references to `topic`, max 3) — filled by editors — without it ONB-07 can't pre-check three topics.
  3. Optional: `appSettings.globeArticles` (SCR-04) (array of references to `article`) — filled by editors — only if the Globe should be curated rather than the latest 32 stories.
- Proposed events: `onboarding_step_viewed` (step), `onboarding_step_skipped` (step), `topic_toggled` (topic, was default), `topics_confirmed` (count, changed from defaults), `notif_preset_selected`, `quiet_hours_changed`, `notif_permission_result` (allowed, denied), `account_method_selected` (apple, google, link, password), `magic_link_sent`, `magic_link_opened`, `account_created` (method), `about_you_completed` (fields given), `onboarding_completed` (signed in or guest, taps).
