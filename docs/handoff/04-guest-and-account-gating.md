## Summary
Guests can do everything without an account (D24). Skipping the account step in onboarding makes a guest whose saves, follows and settings stay on the phone. The app asks for a free account softly: the reader's action finishes first, then a half sheet explains what an account would keep. Asks come at the first share, second save, third follow and first milestone, at most one per session; Not now snoozes asks for 7 days, and after three dismissals only the avatar asks. Readers who signed up with Apple, Google or a magic link but left About you blank have an unfinished profile (D25). Guests without three topics get a For You topic picker, and a cream nudge after three reads in one topic (D26).

## States
- **Guest, fresh:** no asks yet; For You is the picker if fewer than three topics; Saved empty state for guests: Not designed (feature inventory).
- **Ask showing:** backdrop on, gestures paused (NAV-18).
- **Snoozed:** no sheet asks for 7 days. **Three dismissals:** only the avatar and Settings offer the account (board "After dismissing").
- **Unfinished account:** strip until dismissed, then the avatar dot; account sheet tops with Finish your profile.
- **Account created:** guest data moves to the account; strip if the profile is unfinished; asks stop.
- **Permission already declined at the first-follow ask:** the iOS alert won't appear again; native should route to iPhone Settings (see `12-…`). Unclear in prototype.
- **Offline:** guest actions work offline; account creation needs a connection (error not designed).

## Motion
- Ask and notification sheets: rise from 110% below, 320ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; backdrop 250ms.
- Profile strip: fade in 400ms. Topic nudge: rise 16pt and fade, 320ms; auto-hide after 9s.
- Create account on its own: same push and fade as onboarding (400ms); closes with a 400ms fade.
- Reduce Motion: not specified; proposal: fades only.

## Accessibility
- Targets: Create free account 52pt, Not now 44pt, strip dismiss 44pt, nudge Follow 40pt (raise to 44pt), nudge X 40 × 44pt.
- Prototype labels: strip and nudge X "Dismiss"; create-account X "Close".
- Gaps: sheets need modal focus and a heading; the strip's progress needs a spoken value ("2 of 4 done"); the nudge should be announced and must not vanish while VoiceOver focus is on it (pause the 9s timer); the avatar dot needs a spoken state.

## Data and analytics
- All guest state is App state (device) until account creation, then Account (backend). The server must accept a bulk upload of saves, follows, reading progress, settings and notification choices at sign-up.
- Content reads: `topic.title`, `topic.slug.current`, article `topics[]->`, `author.firstName` + `author.lastName`.
- **Missing in schema:** none new. The For You picker depends on the onboarding topic list proposed in `03-onboarding.md` (`appSettings.onboardingTopics` (SCR-04)).
- Proposed events: `guest_created` (from step), `account_ask_shown` (trigger), `account_ask_result` (create, not_now, backdrop), `account_ask_suppressed` (reason: session, snooze, cap), `create_account_opened` (source: ask, avatar, settings), `account_created` (source, method, guest data counts), `profile_strip_shown`, `profile_strip_dismissed`, `profile_completed`, `topic_nudge_shown` (topic), `topic_nudge_result` (follow, dismiss, timeout), `fy_picker_built` (count), `notif_ask_shown` (kind), `notif_ask_result` (allow, deny, not_now).

## Open items
- Follow trigger counts topics plus writers reaching three, including the three pre-checked onboarding topics. A guest who kept the defaults already has three, so the follow ask can only fire after Build my For You or after unfollowing. Decide whether "third follow" means three follows made after onboarding.
- Milestone ask fires at any milestone while eligible; D24 says the first milestone.
- Session definition and on-device persistence of the counters (GATE-02).
- First-follow notification ask runs only for guests, though the onboarding declined screen (ONB-11) promises it to anyone who declined.
- The For You picker also shows for unfinished accounts with fewer than three topics (`fyEmpty` checks "not a full account"); confirm intended.
- Avatar dot shows before the strip is dismissed (GATE-09); D25 says once dismissed.
- Not designed: guest Saved empty state copy, sign in with an existing account from an ask (GATE-13), merging guest data into an existing account, account-creation errors.
